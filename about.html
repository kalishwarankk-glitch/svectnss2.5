About NSS

Not me, but you.

The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a government-backed student volunteer program that connects college students with community service. It gives students practical experience in social responsibility, while helping schools, colleges and local communities benefit directly from organised, well-run programs.

NSS at SVCET

At Sri Venkateshwara College of Engineering and Technology, Ariyur, our NSS unit is run by student volunteers guided by faculty coordinators. We take our programs beyond our own campus — into schools, colleges and public institutions across the region.

🧭

Vision

A generation of students who see service to society as a natural extension of their education — informed, responsible, and ready to act.

🎯

Mission

To design and deliver practical, well-structured awareness and skill programs that genuinely help the schools, colleges and communities that invite us.

Objectives

✔ Build practical awareness on health, safety and environmental responsibility.

✔ Give student volunteers real hands-on experience organising and running programs.

✔ Support schools and colleges that don't have in-house resources for these sessions.

✔ Encourage a habit of service that continues beyond college.

🤝

Student service, in practice

Every NSS program is planned and delivered by our own student volunteers, under faculty guidance — not outsourced, not generic.
