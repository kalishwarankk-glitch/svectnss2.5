For Institutions

Bring NSS to your school or college.

Any school, college or public/community institution can invite our NSS team to run one of our programs.

Who can invite us

Schools, colleges, other educational institutions, and public or community organisations in and around Puducherry are all welcome to request a program.

How the process works

STEP 1
📋

Tell us what you need

Fill the invitation form with your details and program of interest.

STEP 2
📅

We confirm a date

Our coordinator confirms a date and duration.

STEP 3
🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Our volunteers visit

Trained volunteers arrive with everything needed.

STEP 4
🤝

We follow up

We share feedback and discuss future sessions.

What we'll ask for

  • Institution name, type and complete address
  • Name, designation and contact details of the inviting staff member
  • The program you'd like, preferred date and expected duration
  • Approximate number of students attending

Why collaborate with NSS?

You get a ready-made, well-structured session at no cost, delivered by enthusiastic student volunteers.
