Students · Service · Society

SVCET NSS carries the college's service work into schools, colleges and communities.

We're the National Service Scheme unit of Sri Venkateshwara College of Engineering and Technology, Ariyur. Our student volunteers run structured awareness and skill programs — from environment and health to safety and life skills — for any institution that invites us.
